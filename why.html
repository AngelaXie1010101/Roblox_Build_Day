Why join?

Playing is fun. Building your own world is even better.

Build Day helps students move from consuming games to creating them, while learning design, problem-solving, teamwork, and confidence.

Build

What students make

A simple Roblox experience based on the challenge theme, such as an obby, adventure, hangout space, mini-game, or story world.

Learn

What students learn

Roblox Studio basics, game rules, level design, testing, iteration, creative planning, and how to turn an idea into something playable.

Share

What students gain

A finished project, showcase experience, builder confidence, and a creative achievement they can talk about for school applications.

First-time builders welcome

You do not need Roblox Studio experience to join.

If Roblox Studio or coding feels intimidating, that is exactly why Build Day exists. The challenge starts with beginner-friendly activities, simple examples, and small steps so students can learn while they build.

  • No prior coding experience is required.
  • No Roblox Studio experience is required.
  • You can build a simple project and improve it as you learn.

Prizes and recognition

Every builder gets a reason to celebrate.

Build Day gives students a clear creative goal and a moment to be recognized for what they made.

Participation Badge

For every student who submits a finished project by the showcase.

Showcase Spotlight

Selected projects are featured during the online showcase.

Category Awards

Recognition for creativity, teamwork, most fun, best beginner build, and strongest use of the theme.
