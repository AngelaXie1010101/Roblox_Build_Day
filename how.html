How Build Day works

One challenge. A weekend sprint or guided week. One finished Roblox experience.

Roblox Build Day is free, fully online, and made for students who want to try building in Roblox Studio for the first time.

No experience needed

Never coded or used Roblox Studio before? You still belong here.

The program is built for first-time creators. You do not need to know Lua, game design, or Roblox Studio. You will start small, follow guided activities, and learn by making one simple playable experience step by step.

Who can join?

Students from elementary, middle, and high school.

No previous building experience is required. Participants need a Roblox account, access to Roblox Studio, and permission from a parent or guardian when needed.

Before kickoff

  • Create or confirm a Roblox account.
  • Install Roblox Studio on a computer.
  • Decide whether to build solo or with a small team.
  • Register so you receive challenge updates.

Build solo or with friends, then share your finished Roblox experience in the showcase.
