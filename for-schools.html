Bring game creation into the school day.

Roblox Build Day is a structured, beginner-friendly hackathon program that helps students practice design thinking, digital literacy, collaboration, and computational problem-solving through a playable project they are excited to build.

School-day model

A sample schedule that fits a standard school day.

This format gives students a complete build cycle: introduction, skill practice, guided building, testing, and a short showcase. Schools can run it as one school day, a weekend sprint, or a series of shorter sessions.

8:30-9:00

Kickoff and Theme Reveal

Introduce the challenge, examples, expectations, and student learning goals.

9:00-10:15

Roblox Studio Mini Lesson

Learn workspace basics, parts, templates, testing, and saving.

10:30-12:00

Guided Build Sprint

Students create the core of a simple game individually or with a small team.

1:00-2:00

Test and Improve

Students peer test, fix confusing moments, and add small improvements.

2:00-2:45

Showcase and Reflection

Students demo their projects and describe what they built, changed, and learned.

Sample lesson plan

Build a mini challenge game.

Learning Goals

Students understand game objectives, user feedback, testing, iteration, and basic computational logic.

Project Task

Create a simple obstacle or adventure course with a clear start, goal, and win condition.

Facilitator Role

Support checkpoints, ask design questions, help students test, and encourage thoughtful revisions.

Assessment

Use a simple rubric for completion, creativity, usability, reflection, teamwork, and iteration.

Evidence of learning

Students finish with visible work.

  • A playable Roblox Studio project
  • A short design reflection
  • Peer feedback notes or testing observations
  • A final demo or brief presentation

School readiness

Simple setup requirements.

  • Computers that can run Roblox Studio
  • Internet access and supervised work time
  • A teacher, club sponsor, or facilitator
  • Optional family showcase invitation
